# VeylOps — Discord Toolkit

Your Discord control center. Presence, streams and profile, like a pro.
Windows & Android app + web portal: **https://www.veylops.it**

## Features
- **Rich Presence** — show a game, custom status or Spotify track; platform spoofing (mobile, console, VR)
- **Always-on presence & voice** (PRO) — stay online even with the app closed or the PC off
- **Persistent Go Live** — streaming hours from your profile badge
- **Badge Maximizer** — Game Variety & Playtime badges, with a safety cap and cooldown
- **Quests** and multi-account **voice**

## Download
Get the latest **Windows installer** and **Android APK** from the [Releases](https://github.com/kemithereal/veylops-toolkit-discord/releases) page, or from https://www.veylops.it.

> Android APK is debug-signed. Coming from a version older than 1.51.0? Uninstall the old app first.
